What we can do for you

If your team does something repetitive, there's a good chance we can take it over. These are the things we build most often.

Accounts payable

Invoices arrive by email or scan. We read them, match them to the vendor and account, and enter the bill in QuickBooks. A person only sees the exceptions.

Accounts receivable

Invoices go out on schedule, payments get matched, and overdue balances get a polite reminder without anyone chasing them.

QuickBooks & system integrations

QuickBooks connected to your CRM, billing, and operations tools, so nobody types the same numbers twice.

Dashboards & reporting

The numbers you actually use, pulled live from accounting, sales, and operations into one screen, in place of a weekly report built by hand.

Company handbook assistant

Claude connected to your policies, procedures, and files, so anyone can ask a question and get an answer based on your own documents.

Microsoft 365 assistant

An assistant with access to your email, calendar, and files through Microsoft 365, with the right security controls in place.

Quotes, proposals & emails

Shortcuts for the writing you repeat every week, like quotes, proposals, and customer emails, drafted in your company's style.

CRM build-outs

A CRM set up around how your team actually sells and serves customers, with follow-ups automated and accounting connected.

Common questions

What happens in the free workflow review?

It's a 30-minute call or screen share. You show us a process that takes up your team's time, we ask questions, and we tell you honestly whether AI or automation can take it over. There's no cost and no obligation.

What kinds of businesses do you work with?

All kinds. We aren't tied to one industry. Our clients range from small teams to larger organizations, and what they have in common is repetitive work that eats up people's time.
