// what we've built

Real work, running for real businesses.

A few of the systems we've built for clients. If your team does something repetitive, rules-based, or stuck in a spreadsheet, there's a good chance we can automate it.

Accounts payable automation

Invoices arrive by email, PDF, or scan. The system reads them, matches each one to the right vendor and account, and enters the bill, so your AP work runs itself.

  • Invoice capture and data entry
  • Matching to vendors, POs, and GL codes
  • Only the exceptions go to a person

Accounts receivable automation

Invoicing, payment matching, and follow-up on overdue balances, handled automatically and on schedule, so cash comes in without someone chasing it by hand.

  • Invoices generated and sent on time
  • Payments matched and applied
  • Polite, automatic reminders

QuickBooks integrations

Connect QuickBooks to the rest of your business so data flows both ways, with no more copying numbers between systems or re-keying the same entry twice.

  • Sync with your CRM, billing, or ops tools
  • Automatic entries and reconciliation
  • Clean data your accountant will like

Dashboards & reporting

One screen showing the numbers that matter, pulled live from your accounting, sales, and operations systems, in place of a weekly scramble to build reports.

  • Live data from the tools you use
  • Built around the decisions you make
  • Automated reports to your inbox

CRM build-outs

A CRM set up around how your team actually sells and serves customers, with pipelines, automations, and integrations configured and ready to use.

  • Custom pipelines and fields
  • Follow-ups and tasks automated
  • Connected to accounting and email

// faq

Common questions.

Don't see yours? Call or email us. We're happy to talk it through, with no pressure.

What kinds of businesses do you work with?

All kinds. We aren't tied to one industry. Our clients range from small teams to larger organizations, and what they share is repetitive work that eats up their people's time. If that sounds like you, we should talk.

How quickly can we see something?

For most projects, we can map out the solution and show you a working demo within about two weeks. Full rollout depends on the size of the project, and we'll give you a realistic timeline up front.

How much does a project cost?

It depends on scope. A single automation is very different from a full custom platform. After a short conversation, we'll give you a clear quote for what we'd build.

Do you work with QuickBooks and the tools we already use?

Yes. We build QuickBooks integrations regularly, and we work with Microsoft 365, SharePoint, CRMs, and most systems that have an API or a web interface. We work with what you already have and don't make you switch.

Is our data safe?

Security is part of the design from day one. C7 Intelligence is part of Cyber7 Group, which also provides managed IT and cybersecurity. Before anything is built, we'll walk you through where your data goes, who can access it, and how it's protected.

Are you local? Do you work remotely?

Both. Many of our clients are in the Charlotte area, and we work with businesses across the country. Most projects run smoothly with remote meetings and screen shares.

What if AI isn't the right fit for our problem?

Then we'll tell you. Sometimes the better answer is a simple automation, a software fix, or a process change. We'd rather give you the honest answer than sell you something that won't work.

What's eating your team's week?

Tell us the process. We'll tell you honestly whether AI or automation can take it over, and then show you with a working demo.
