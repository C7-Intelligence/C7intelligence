What does C7 Intelligence mean by AI integration?

C7 Intelligence connects an AI system to the tools a company already uses, with testing and access controls, so the result can run in production rather than as a demo.

Example on the site

One example is an AI that reads customer email, pulls order details, and creates an order for a person to approve.

The tools named for this work are Azure AI Foundry, OpenAI, and Claude.

How it starts

The site offers a free 30-minute workflow review and says a working demo takes about two weeks. The path is Discover, Demo, Build, Scale.

Common questions

Is this a chatbot project?

Not only. The site includes assistants, but the integration offer is AI connected to business systems and workflows.
